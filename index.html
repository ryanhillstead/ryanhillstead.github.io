R—H · What I do

R—H · Off the clock

I build software for the people who power The Grid.

I'm a software developer in Spanish Fork, Utah, working in the power industry — building web apps and internal tools for the people who generate and deliver electricity across the West. Off the clock I'm usually tinkering with a side project or out in the Utah desert.

Off the clock I'm a husband, a dad, and an F1 fan.

Most of my week outside work belongs to my family. What's left goes to Formula 1 and video games — and to the side projects below.

Power systems, now

Internal tools and web apps for the people who generate and deliver electricity. It runs against real operational data, so correctness and traceability matter more than novelty — when a tool is wrong, someone downstream has to catch it.

Vision AI, before that

Annotated video and sensor data from a production vehicle fleet to train the machine-learning models behind driver assistance, working with data engineers to shape annotation requirements and iterate on the strategy behind them.

Business intelligence

Queried and cleaned raw data out of Oracle and Azure sources, built dashboards in Power BI, Tableau and Excel, and migrated a body of Oracle SQL to Azure T-SQL without losing the queries behind it.
