Selected work

Operations, not demos

The software I write in this industry runs against real operational data. When a tool is wrong, someone downstream has to catch it, so correctness and traceability matter more than novelty.

Internal tools

Most of it is the unglamorous middle: moving data between systems that were never designed to talk, turning spreadsheets into something queryable, and giving people interfaces they can trust.

Built to be handed over

These tools outlive the sprint that produced them. I write them to be read and maintained by whoever comes next, not just to work today.
